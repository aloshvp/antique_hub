Privacy Policy

Privacy & Data Protection

Last updated: {{ last_updated|default:"October 2026" }}

At Antique Hub, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This policy briefly explains what data we collect and how we keep it safe.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect basic details required to manage your account, process acquisitions, and deliver items:

  • Account Details: Your name, email, phone number, and delivery address.
  • Order Records: History of your purchases, invoices, and delivery confirmations.
  • Dealer Credentials: Official credentials submitted by antique dealers for verification.

2. How We Protect Your Data

  • No Selling of Data: We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.
  • Secure Payments: Transactions are handled through encrypted payment gateways; card numbers are never stored on our servers.
  • Encryption: All platform traffic is secured using 256-bit TLS encryption.

3. Provenance & Compliance

All marketplace items comply with the Antiquities and Art Treasures Act, 1972 for legal domestic ownership in India. Every acquired artifact includes an authenticity certificate and a 7-day inspection period upon delivery.

4. Your Rights

You can review, update, or request the deletion of your account and personal details anytime through your profile or by contacting our team.

If you have any questions regarding your data or privacy, contact us anytime:
